Mathematical Catalog¶

Exact Symbolic Synthesis & Historical Lineage — Inspired by H. E. Huntley (1970)

Reference Work: The Divine Proportion: A Study in Mathematical Beauty (Dover Original, 1970, xii + 186 pp., ISBN 978-0-486-22254-7)
Author: Herbert Edwin (“Ted”) Huntley, F.Inst.P. (1892 – 1975)
Notation: \(\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\) and signed conjugate \(\phi' = \frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\) (\(\phi + \phi' = 1\), \(\phi \cdot \phi' = -1\))
Digital Synthesis: GEOMETOR (100% Exact SymPy Algebra, geometor.model Constructions & Cross-Corpus Concordance)

Published as an original Dover paperback in June 1970, H. E. Huntley’s The Divine Proportion established the modern English anthology of elementary golden-ratio geometry—directly inspiring Dr. Ron Knott (1996, geometor/knott), Alexander Bogomolny (Cut-the-Knot, geometor/divine), and Alfred S. Posamentier & Ingmar Lehmann (2011, geometor/posamentier).

\(\phi^n = F_n \phi + F_{n-1}\), continued fraction [1; 1, 1, ...], and nested radical (HUNT-04.01).

Whirling-square Golden Rectangle (HUNT-05.01) and Huntley’s Golden Ellipse (p. 65, HUNT-05.02: \(a=\phi, b=1, c=\sqrt{\phi}\), forming Kepler’s Right Triangle!).

Poincaré and Hardy criteria of inevitability and economy.

Nested pentagrams, regular decagon, and 3 mutually orthogonal golden rectangles spanning the 12 vertices of the Icosahedron & Dodecahedron (HUNT-08.01).

Patterns

The Fibonacci Numbers

137–147

Binet’s formula, Lucas sequence \(L_n = \phi^n + (\phi')^n\), Cassini’s identity, and \(\sum F_i^2 = F_n F_{n+1}\) rectangle tiling (HUNT-11.01).

XII

Nature’s Golden Numbers

148–163

Two-plate glass internal light reflections (\(F_{k+2}\) paths, HUNT-12.01), phyllotaxis, Golden Angle, and drone bee genealogy (HUNT-12.02).

XIII

Spira Mirabilis

164–182

Bernoulli’s equiangular logarithmic spiral, whirling golden triangle pole, and evolute/pedal self-similarity (HUNT-13.01).
